FinNextAssist decomposes the research process into four stages: Task Planner, Evidence Compiler, Reasoning Engine, and Report Assembler, and introduces two novel lightweight sub-agents: TabAgent, for cross-market financial table understanding, and HeteroAgent, for cross-modality heterogeneous financial data interpretation. Extensive experiments on FinDeepResearch, the Finance Agent Benchmark, and FinTMMBench-Web show that FinNextAssist substantially outperforms both strong proprietary and open-source DR agents, with ablation studies confirming the contribution of each component across diverse markets and languages.
